Mock vs Build: the 37 differences, one screen at a time

Each screen below shows the mock (left) and the app as it is today (right), at phone width in the dark theme, then the differences between them in plain words, numbered 1 to 37 as in conformance.md. Nothing is decided: all 37 are back to proposed.

Your rule: the screens look like the mocks. So the recommendation on every item is to follow the mock, except four where the app does something the mock never drew. Those four are marked Your call.

What still wins over a mock: colours, sizes, radii and glyphs come from the token and icon documents (the mocks are dark-only, so their hex values do not settle the light theme); text must still reflow at 200% text size; a name may end in an ellipsis as in the mocks.
