# Questions for the owner: ledger and income (13 November 2026)

## Already asked and answered in chat, 13 November 2026

These are recorded in `docs/design/ledger-income.md` §0.

1. **The name.** Label and route: Money, at `#/money`, with `#/expenses` redirecting.
2. **A recurring salary.** It reminds you and posts only when you confirm.
3. **A bonus.** Answered by changing FIRE's rule. Instead of the highest month, FIRE uses
   the usual amount, the one that recurs, so a bonus month does not move it.
4. **Money back** (a reimbursement, a friend repaying). It is "Refund or money back": it
   takes away from spent and is not income.
5. **Business income.** Money records what reaches home: money drawn from the business
   into a household account. FIRE uses the last 12 complete months' total, or the
   average × 12 when there are fewer months. Tax takes a typed net profit.
6. **A mixed account.** Left as it is: client fees paid into a personal account are
   Business income, and business costs paid from it count as spent.

## Answered in chat, 13 November 2026, each by accepting the proposal

7. **The usual salary:** the last six months entered; on a tie the more recent amount;
   when nothing repeats, the latest month, said so.
8. **Loan EMIs:** a fourth part of the split, *paid to loans*, and SC-I12 gains it.
9. **Another member's tax inputs:** read by that member's login and by whoever typed them.
10. **The audit log:** audit rows for private income follow the visibility of the row they
    record. CLAUDE.md's audit paragraph needs this as a named exception.
